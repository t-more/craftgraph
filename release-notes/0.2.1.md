# CraftGraph 0.2.1

A small release on top of [0.2](0.2.md).

- **Marked experimental.** A red "EXPERIMENTAL" label sits on the window (hover it for what that
  means) and at the top of the readme. The addon has now been tried in the game, but not for
  long: check what it proposes before you spend gold, and say what breaks.
- **Versions are semantic from here on**: major.minor.patch. The update check already compares
  all three numbers.
- **A trailer**, for fun: `docs/media/trailer.mp4`. Made entirely from the addon's own pictures,
  a speech synthesiser and tone generators.

## Still true
- Experimental; tried in the game only briefly.
- The Windows installer, importer and setup launcher have not been run on a Windows machine.
- The hourly Blizzard prices have been tested against a copy of the service, not the live one.
- The Take button for mail has not been tried in the game.
- Profits take the auction house cut off every auction sale. The higher cut of a neutral auction
  house and listing deposits are not counted.
