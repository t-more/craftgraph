# CraftGraph 0.3.0

Money in the plan window, and estimates that start from the market. Follows
[0.2.1](0.2.1.md).

- **What a plan costs and brings.** In the plan window, each item shows what one costs to make
  or buy and what it sells for, and the same for all of them. A **Whole plan** line at the
  bottom gives what the plan costs, what is still to spend, and what it brings after the cut.
- **Fixed: planned trades were turned into crafts.** If you planned to relist an item, sell it
  to a vendor or buy it from a vendor, and the item has a recipe, the plan gave crafting steps.
  It now buys the item.
- **Margin over time.** A sixth tick box under "Show ..." draws, in pink, what making one and
  selling it left at each scan. Beside it are where it is heading and how sure that is.
- **Estimates start from the market.**
  - A new item's daily wander and hour-of-day effect start from what the realm's other items do.
  - The selling rate starts from the share of stock this market sells a day.
  - `/cg priors` prints them.
  - Measured: with 2 to 4 days of history on the simulated market, about a tenth less error.
    No difference yet on 15 hours of real scans.
- **Surer signals.** The sell signal only says "now" or "wait" when it is at least three chances
  in four. "If prices turn" says honestly that on real scans it ends worse roughly one time in
  ten.
- Chart labels no longer overlap at the top.
- The trailer was remade: 35 seconds, a composed track, and a neural narrator when Kokoro is
  installed.
- The readme has a "not financial advice" picture at the top.
