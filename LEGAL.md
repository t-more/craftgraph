## Sharing prices with other players
Sharing is optional and by your own choice. What you share (your character's name, your GitHub
name if you use the shared repository, and prices from your own in-game scans) becomes visible to
whoever you share it with, or to everyone in the case of the public repository.

## Privacy
CraftGraph collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere by itself. Everything it stores
is on your computer: the game's saved file, and for the optional tool a database and your key in
your own folders. The auction data from Blizzard contains no player names. The update check asks
GitHub for the latest version number and sends nothing else. Whatever you share, you share
yourself.
