# CraftGraph 0.2

Install over 0.1 the same way. **Quit the game fully and start it again**: this version adds
files, so `/cg reload` is not enough. Your saved prices carry over and are repaired once at login.

## Fixed
- **Full scans were recorded piece by piece.** The game sends the full list in pieces and each
  piece was taken for a whole scan, so anything not yet arrived was written down as "none
  listed" and counted as sold. That gave wild "sells N a day" figures and false gaps in the
  history. The list is now read once it has all arrived. Old data is repaired at login: the
  false "none listed" points are removed and the sales figures start again.
- Scans a few minutes apart no longer replace each other in the history.
- Buying from an opportunity refuses when the listings now cost more than the row counted on.
- A full scan that gets no answer no longer holds up the item lookups.
- Forecasts start from the present after hours away from the auction house.
- Four-column graphs fit their view.

## New
- **Pages.** The buttons along the top are fixed (Graph, Opportunities, Sell bags, Trends, Data)
  and the one in view is lit. With no item chosen the window opens on Opportunities.
- **Data page** with four sub-pages: Data sources, Share prices, Online sharing, Sales seen.
- **Sales seen.** How fast things sell is judged from auctions that vanish between full scans;
  every auction judged sold is listed with its item, stack, price, seller and how sure. Click
  "Supply and demand" in the side panel for one item's.
- **Sell when.** Each opportunity says whether now is a good time to sell (now / any time / wait),
  from the last three hours against the usual price of the last week.
- **Sharing prices.** Export your latest scan as text and import a friend's with one paste, or
  bring in a `.cg.json` file. Optionally pool prices in a shared GitHub repository through the
  Python tool. Each player who shares with you is a data source with a switch of their own; your
  own shared prices are never imported back.
- **Price chart.** "Show ..." opens tick boxes: a grid with prices and percentages (on), change
  bars, the certainty glow, supply (units listed) and demand (sold a day, with its scale). A
  smoothing period under the chart, from 15 minutes to a day.
- **Graph.** Lines are gold where a step earns and red where it loses, thicker towards the
  bigger earners, with the profit of one craft and faint lights drifting the way the money flows
  (`/cg flow` switches them off). Zoomed out, cards show a larger name and one line; zoom in for
  everything.
- **What to make.** Every craftable card is scored and the best on screen gets a golden rim that
  breathes; the rest are tinted by how they compare. Things made from the item are grouped (make
  now / buy first / recipe not known, greyed) and sorted best first. Rank by the whole amount or
  per craft, counted cautiously (the estimate less its margin), and set with a slider how much
  you are willing to buy at auction.
- **Recipes not learned.** An item you have the profession for but cannot make yet carries a
  small mark on its icon: the item that teaches the recipe, with where to get it and its price.
  The addon now knows the teaching item of about 1,500 recipes, and tracks their prices.
- **The side panel leads with the answer**: what making it earns, then what that rests on.
- **Trends** opens with four short lines: Now, Trend, Sells, When.
- **Forecasts are held back when they would be guesses**, with the reason; `/cg guesses` shows
  them anyway.
- **History is kept in full inside the game**, packed small: every scan for two days, hourly for
  three weeks, six-hourly for four months, daily after.
- **Blizzard prices page** (Data, or `/cg blizzard`): whether the hourly data is connected, the
  setup steps to copy, and how old the oldest data is. Blizzard's terms allow thirty days; the
  page and a login line remind you, and a button (and the tool's `purge`) deletes what is older.
  Nothing is deleted without you.
- **A setup page for the online features.** With Python 3, double-click "Set up online features"
  in the `sync` folder (or `sh sync/setup.sh`): a page in your browser, on your computer only,
  to read what you take on, enter your own API key, fetch prices now or hourly, delete old data,
  keep every scan and share prices. The key goes to one private file outside the addon folder.
- **Your responsibility.** `LEGAL.md`, the tool and the page say plainly that this is a prototype
  among friends, that the key and Blizzard's terms are your own, and what breaking them can cost.
- `/cg version`. A newer release is announced at login when the optional tool has looked for
  one; the Windows installer says so too.

## Clearer words
"Plan it" everywhere, "usual price", "costs N to make", "rose" and "fell" in the plan window,
"Best amount" with "Set", margins as coins. Sources that are not set up or installed have no tick.

## Still true
- Most of this has been checked outside the game only. Auto scan has run in the game without
  errors; the rest has not been seen there yet.
- The Windows installer, importer and setup launcher have not been run on a Windows machine.
- The hourly Blizzard prices have been tested against a copy of the service, not the live one.
- Profits take the auction house cut off every auction sale. The higher cut of a neutral auction
  house and listing deposits are not counted.
- The sales figure reads too low for busy items scanned hours apart.
